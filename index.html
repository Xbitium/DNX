Why DNX?

The Internet is full of workarounds.

DNS, NAT, VPNs, firewalls, and zero-trust layers all solve problems that the network itself does not understand. DNX explores a simpler model: identity at the addressing layer.

The idea

  • The network should recognize names.
  • Ownership should be cryptographic.
  • Deployment should be incremental.
